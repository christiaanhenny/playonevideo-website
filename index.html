Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered.

What is PlayOneVideo?

PlayOneVideo is an iOS app that lets parents choose exactly one YouTube video for their child to watch. When the video ends, the screen goes black. No autoplay, no recommendations, no scrolling — just the one video you chose.

Is this better than YouTube Kids?

YouTube Kids still shows endless suggestions and autoplay. PlayOneVideo gives you complete control — you pick the video, the child watches it, the screen turns off. No feed, no recommendations, no way to click to something else.

How much does PlayOneVideo cost?

PlayOneVideo starts with a 14-day free trial, then €1.99 per month. One subscription covers your whole family — your partner gets access too, at no extra charge.

Does PlayOneVideo collect data about my child?

No. PlayOneVideo only stores a user ID, a nickname for your child, and their saved video list. No personal data, no behavioral tracking, no ads.

Which phones does it work on?

PlayOneVideo is currently available on iPhone and iPad (iOS). An Android version is in development and coming soon.
